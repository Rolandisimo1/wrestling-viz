Recruit Explorer: High-School Freestyle Talent vs. NCAA Payoff

Every dot is a wrestler who placed at Fargo and later competed at the NCAAs. Above the trend line = overachieved; below = underachieved.

X-axis: Best Fargo freestyle finish (left = 8th, right = national champion). Y-axis: total NCAA points contributed (advancement + bonus + placement — credits every match won, not just All-American finishes). Green dots exceeded what their Fargo rank predicted; red dots fell short. Only wrestlers who placed at Fargo and reached the NCAAs are shown. Note: Fargo rank uses freestyle placement only (freestyle predicts NCAA success better than Greco or the two combined); Wrestlers whose school discontinued wrestling during their eligibility (e.g. LSU 1985, Georgia ~1981) are greyed out — their zero NCAA points reflect a lost program, not underachievement. Wrestlers with titles in divisions we don't cover (e.g. Cadet) may be understated.
