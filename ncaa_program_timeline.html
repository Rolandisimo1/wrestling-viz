How to read this & what the numbers mean ▾
The light-green line is recruiting-class rank — incoming talent as a percentile among these programs (left axis, 0–1), since talent has no natural unit. The dark-green line is NCAA team points — the program's actual total team score at the national tournament each year (right axis, real points). The bars show over/under-performance: green-up = wrestlers outperformed what their high-school résumé predicted (model value-added), red-down = they underperformed. Dashed verticals = head-coaching changes.

Recruiting-class rank is built from national high-school résumé (rankings + Junior-national placement + age-group World medals) (not recruiting-service stars), counting only wrestlers who placed (top-8) at the national high-school pipeline and later qualified for NCAAs — recruits with no national high-school footprint in the ranking/placement sources, or never reached NCAAs, are excluded. NCAA team points are the official team score where a program finished top-10 (team place shown in the tooltip); in years a program finished outside the top-10, points are a calibrated estimate (modern-era validation r=0.97) and place is shown as "outside top-10". Team points reflect the whole roster, including transfers and wrestlers who scored without All-American finishes.
